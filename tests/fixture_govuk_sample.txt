## [National River Walks](https://find-government-grants.service.gov.uk/grants/national-river-walks-1)
Defra is looking to award grants via the National River Walks Programme to address the Environmental Improvement Plan 2025 commitment to deliver 9 new national river walks, one in each region of England. One river walk has already been delivered in the North West region. We now welcome applications from the remaining 8 regions of England to deliver the next river walks.

Location
England

Funding organisation
Department for Environment Food and Rural Affairs

Who can apply
Public Sector, Non-profit, Private Sector, Local authority

How much you can get
From £1 to £1.35 million

Total size of grant scheme
£5.4 million

Opening date
2 July 2026, 2:00pm

Closing date
27 August 2026, 12:00pm (Midday)

## [Barnsley AI Upskilling Fund](https://find-government-grants.service.gov.uk/grants/barnsley-ai-upskilling-fund-1)
The Barnsley AI Upskilling Fund is available for upskilling providers to deliver novel AI skills pilots in Barnsley, aiming to drive SME productivity and support local labour market participation and progression as part of the Tech Town initiative.

Location
North West England

Funding organisation
Department for Science, Innovation and Technology

Who can apply
Public Sector, Non-profit, Private Sector

How much you can get
From £100,000 to £350,000

Total size of grant scheme
£800,000

Opening date
15 July 2026, 12:00pm (Midday)

Closing date
12 August 2026, 5:00pm
